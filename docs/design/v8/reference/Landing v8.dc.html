WHERE IT FAILS, IN THE OPEN

We publish where the method gets it wrong.

At Barh, India, the rule that caught 87.5% of new water also flagged 32.6% of water that was already there. A radar change is evidence, not a verdict. We never guess a cause, a probability or a depth.

Two radars, one mission

NISAR carries an L-band radar built by NASA's JPL and an S-band radar from ISRO. It launched from Sriharikota on 30 July 2025.

A dish the width of a house

Its 12 m reflector of gold-plated wire mesh unfolded in orbit in August 2025. It focuses the radar on a strip of ground about 240 km wide.

Open data, every pass

Every product goes to NASA's archive at the Alaska Satellite Facility, free for anyone. NISAR Hazard Watch reads that archive and turns it into maps.
