06 · TWO PASSES, ONE PHASE

Fringes draw how the radar's path changed.

Interferometry compares the phase of two echoes from the same orbit. Each fringe is about 12 cm of change in the radar's path length: half of the 24 cm wave, because the pulse travels there and back. The path changes when the ground moves and when the air between ground and satellite changes, so the explorer shows coherence and wrapped phase, measured as they are.
